Two careers, one habit

I spent ten years in IT operations management, running support desks and keeping the infrastructure other people relied on invisible — until it broke.

These days I'm behind the wheel of an HGV, and the same instinct that made me a decent IT manager keeps showing up: when a process is fiddly or a rule is confusing, I'd rather build a small tool than keep muddling through. Everything on this site was built with Claude Code, most of it in a single evening per tool. I'm slowly turning that habit into a small self-employed business.
