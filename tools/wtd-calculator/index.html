    How this works

    • Average working time must not exceed 48 hours a week, averaged over your reference period (17 weeks standard, or 26 with a workforce agreement) — the average here only counts weeks you've actually logged within that window.
    • If your working time exceeds 6 hours in a day, you're entitled to a break of at least 30 minutes; over 9 hours, at least 45 minutes (breaks can be taken in blocks of 15 minutes or more).
    • On any day that includes night work (any time between midnight and 4am), working time is capped at 10 hours unless a workforce agreement says otherwise.
    • "Working time" is broader than driving — it includes loading, unloading, cleaning, paperwork and other duties, but not breaks or genuine waiting/availability time.

    This covers the core rules for mobile workers under the Road Transport (Working Time) Regulations 2005. Self-employed drivers are generally outside scope of these specific limits (though the tachograph driving-time rules still apply), and this doesn't account for every exemption or workforce agreement variation. Always check your own contract and your company's compliance guidance — this tool isn't a substitute for either.
