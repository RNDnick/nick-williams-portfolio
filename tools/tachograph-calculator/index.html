    How this works

    • After a maximum of 4.5 hours of driving, you need a break of at least 45 minutes before driving again.
    • That break can be split: a first part of at least 15 minutes, followed later by a second part of at least 30 minutes, in that order.
    • A break under 15 minutes doesn't count towards either rule — the driving clock keeps running.
    • Standard daily driving is capped at 9 hours, extendable to 10 hours on up to two days a week.

    This covers the core EU/GB drivers' hours rules for driving time and breaks. It doesn't account for every exemption (multi-manning, ferry/train crossings, AETR international journeys, weekly rest and the 90-hour/two-week limit) — always check your company's compliance guidance and your tachograph record for the full picture. This tool isn't a substitute for either.
